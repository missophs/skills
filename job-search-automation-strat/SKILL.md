# Job Search Automation Strategy

Help design and execute a systematic, automated job search. Treat the job search like a pipeline: source → filter → outreach → follow-up → convert.

## Framework

**1. Define the target** — role, level, company size, industry, location/remote, comp range. No target = spray and pray = low yield.

**2. Source channels** — rank by signal quality: warm intros > direct outreach to hiring managers > niche job boards > LinkedIn/Indeed. Don't treat all channels equally.

**3. Build the pipeline** — track every opportunity: company, role, source, status, next action, date. Use a simple tracker (Notion, Airtable, spreadsheet).

**4. Automate the repeatable parts** — job alerts (LinkedIn, Wellfound, role-specific boards), email templates for outreach, follow-up sequences, application autofill.

**5. Outreach** — personalized cold outreach to hiring managers and referrals beats cold applying. Template + 3 lines of genuine personalization.

**6. Metrics** — track: applications sent, response rate, interview rate, offer rate. Optimize the lowest-converting stage.

## Rules

- Quality over quantity. 20 targeted applications outperform 200 spray-and-pray.
- Follow up. One email is not enough. Three touches over two weeks is the minimum.
- Referrals 10x interview rates. Prioritize activating your network before mass applying.
- Ask what stage of the search they're in before advising — the strategy differs.
