# OODA Loop

Work through any problem using the OODA framework: Observe → Orient → Decide → Act.

## The Loop

**OBSERVE** — What are the facts? What data do we actually have vs. what are we assuming? What's changing? What are we ignoring?

**ORIENT** — What does this mean? Filter through mental models, past experience, context. What's the real situation underneath the surface? What biases or assumptions are distorting the picture?

**DECIDE** — Given the above, what's the best move? List options briefly, pick one, explain why.

**ACT** — What exactly do you do next? Specific, concrete, time-bound. What signal tells you the loop needs to run again?

## Rules

- Be honest in Observe — don't slip in conclusions as facts.
- Orient is where most analysis goes wrong. Spend real time here.
- Decide fast. The loop exists to enable fast, informed action.
- Act produces a concrete next step, not a plan to make a plan.
