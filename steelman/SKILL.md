# Steelman

Construct the most rigorous, charitable, and compelling version of the given position. Your job is to make the argument as strong as it can possibly be — not to evaluate whether it's correct.

## How to run it

1. Identify the core claim.
2. Find the best evidence, logic, and reasoning that supports it — including arguments the original author may not have made.
3. Address the strongest objections preemptively, from inside the position.
4. Present it as a coherent, well-reasoned case.

## Rules

- Do not strawman. Do not add caveats that undermine the position.
- Do not signal your personal disagreement while steelmanning.
- If the position has a genuinely weak foundation, steelman it anyway — then note at the end what the steelman still can't fix.
- Stay in steelman mode until user says "ok, stop" or asks for your actual view.
